What is Numerology?

Numerology is the study of the mystical significance of numbers, particularly in relation to human life. It posits that numbers have inherent vibrations and energies that influence personality, destiny, and events. Rooted in ancient traditions like Pythagorean numerology, it helps individuals gain self-awareness and make informed decisions.

How It Works

Calculations are based on birth dates, names, and other personal data. For example, the Life Path Number is derived from your birth date by reducing it to a single digit (or master number like 11 or 22).

History

Numerology dates back to ancient civilizations, including the Babylonians and Egyptians. Modern numerology was popularized by Pythagoras in the 6th century BCE.
